Yas Marina 2026 Technical Profile

Yas Marina is a twilight-to-night race that finishes the season under the lights. Under the 2026 power unit and active aero rules, the long back straight drains the battery hard, and the slow, traction-heavy final sector around the marina punishes poor energy deployment.

  • Overtaking hotspots: the Turn 1 braking zone and the Turn 5 hairpin at the end of the back straight.
  • Aero: medium-downforce setups, using straight mode on the long straights and corner mode elsewhere.
  • Tyres: Pirelli C3 (Hard), C4 (Medium), C5 (Soft), as in 2025.

2026 Formula 1 Regulations

The 2026 FIA Technical Regulations are the biggest rules change in modern open-wheel racing:

Near 50/50 Power Split

The 1.6L V6 turbo produces roughly 400 kW, while the MGU-K output rises to 350 kW. The MGU-H is removed.

100% Sustainable Fuel

Engines run on advanced sustainable fuel with no fossil-carbon content.

Active Aerodynamics

Movable front and rear wings switch between a high-downforce corner mode and a low-drag straight mode, replacing the DRS flap.

Smaller, Lighter Cars

Width falls to 1900 mm (down 100 mm), wheelbase to 3400 mm (down 200 mm), and minimum weight to 768 kg (down 30 kg).
